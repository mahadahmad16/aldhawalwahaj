OUR HISTORY

Company Profile

Al Dhaw Al Wahaj Electric and Sanitary Ware Trading LLC was established from scratch on 25 March 2012. Through constant hard work and dedication, we have become a well-known fan importer, wholesaler and distributor in the UAE and other GCC countries.

I have been part of the same business, Industrial Fans Sales, since April 2000 in Dubai, UAE. In 2012, we established a new company in Sharjah, UAE: Al Dhaw Al Wahaj Electric and Sanitary Ware Trading LLC. We initially became the sole distributor of General Fan Company Pvt. Ltd. (GFC Fans), a well-renowned Pakistani fan company. Later, we began importing fans from five Pakistani companies and four Chinese companies.

We currently serve more than 225 wholesale customers in the UAE and export directly from Pakistan to Iraq, Qatar, Sudan and Yemen.

Our motto is to deliver quality products with prompt after-sales service.
